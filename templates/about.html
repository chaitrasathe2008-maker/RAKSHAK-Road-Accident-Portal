🚑 RAKSHAK

🚑 About RAKSHAK

🚨 Our Project

RAKSHAK is a Road Accident Emergency Portal designed to help people report road accidents quickly and access emergency assistance.

🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of RAKSHAK is to provide a simple platform for accident reporting and emergency assistance.

⚙️ Main Features

🚨 Accident Reporting
🗄️ Accident Report Database
👮 Police Dashboard
🏥 Hospital Information
🚑 Emergency Services
📞 Emergency Contact
